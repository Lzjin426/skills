# Quality Rubric

Use this file before final delivery.

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | 5 means | Warning signs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning path | The reader moves from problem to intuition to formal detail smoothly | Headings are a keyword list |
| Accuracy | Definitions, formulas, dates, and claims are source-backed | Unsourced claims or stale facts |
| Depth | Key concepts are explained where readers usually get stuck | Many sections are only 2-3 lines |
| Terminology | Terms are introduced with role and context | Terms appear without explanation |
| Examples | Examples carry abstraction and show operations | Examples are decorative or unrelated |
| Visuals | Diagrams/images teach structure or mechanism | Images are decorative or unlicensed |
| Source use | Sources are compared and attributed | One source dominates the article |
| Anti-AI style | Prose is coherent, specific, and non-formulaic | Bullet soup, vague praise, generic transitions |
| Boundaries | Limitations and misconceptions are clear | Article sounds universally true |
| Practical value | Reader knows how to continue learning or use the concept | Ends with a generic summary |

## Acceptance Gate

Do not deliver a substantial article until these are true:

- There is a source inventory or references section.
- The lead can stand alone.
- The outline has fewer, deeper H2 sections unless the topic genuinely requires many.
- Every formula or technical term has context.
- At least one example, diagram, table, or visual plan exists when the concept is abstract.
- External images have attribution/licensing notes or are replaced with self-drawn diagrams.
- The final draft has been reviewed for shallow headings and AI-like phrasing.

## Final Review Questions

Ask:

1. What is the one-sentence mental model?
2. What is the first place a beginner would get stuck?
3. Does the article slow down there?
4. Which source supports the most important technical claim?
5. Which section could be merged or deleted?
6. Which visual is essential, and which is decorative?
7. What would be misleading if the reader stopped halfway?

## Delivery Notes

When giving the result to the user, mention:

- What sources shaped the article.
- Any uncertainty or source conflict.
- Which external images were used and why.
- Whether the article is ready for private notes, team knowledge base, or public publishing.
